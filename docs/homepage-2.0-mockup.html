The barn / Øhavsladen

Raising a barn the old way

On South Fyn we are rebuilding an 1850s barn as a hand-cut timber frame workshop, with axes, chisels, and The Northmen. It will become a place to teach the craft. Follow the build here and on ohavsladen.dk.
